# Content Review Policy

Every content package must define how its learning materials are reviewed before publication.
Reviewers check factual accuracy, completeness, consistency, accessibility, and source attribution
for the subject area being taught.

## Review Requirements

- A reviewer other than the author approves content before it is published.
- Reviewers have relevant subject-matter knowledge or consult a qualified reviewer when needed.
- Facts, statistics, quotations, and recommendations cite credible sources or are identified as opinions.
- Each lesson records `accuracy_reviewed_by` and `accuracy_reviewed_at` metadata.
- Package-specific legal notices and review requirements are maintained with that package.

## Corrections

- Record a reported issue in the instance's issue tracker and assign an owner.
- Link the correction to the affected content and its review record.
- Re-review changed material before publishing it.
- Document any delay when an issue requires additional research or external review.

See the [content review checklist](./content-accuracy-checklist.md).
